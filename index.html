EARTH / SOUTH INDIA / A LIVING TRADITION

Who invented
Carnatic music?

Here's the surprising part: there is no single known inventor.

But meet Purandara Dasa (c. 1484–1564), traditionally called the father of Carnatic music for helping organize the graded exercises singers still learn today. He shaped a way of teaching an already centuries-old tradition.

AN EIGHT-HUNDRED-YEAR QUESTION? OLDER STILL.

Seven swaras.
Endless possibilities.

Sa · Ri · Ga · Ma · Pa · Dha · Ni

Why practise the same notes over and over? Sarali Varisai develops pitch awareness; repeated-note exercises build control; rhythmic exercises train timing. Not magic changes to your vocal cords—learned coordination between listening, breathing and singing.
